FARM HOUSE MODEL — prompts & workflow (Part 2 placeholder)
==========================================================

Intended pipeline: 2D concept (gen-AI image) -> 3D conversion -> optimization -> farm-house.glb

2D concept prompt (image gen):
  "Cozy cartoon farm house in the style of Klondike Adventure commercials,
   cream plaster walls, warm red pitched roof, wooden door, small round
   windows, stone chimney, chunky exaggerated proportions, hand-painted
   look, bright cheerful colors, isometric 3/4 view, game asset concept art"

3D conversion: Meshy / Tripo AI image-to-3D, then Blender cleanup + retopo.

Optimization targets:
  - < 3,000 triangles
  - single 512x512 texture atlas
  - exported as GLB, compressed
  - origin at ground center

Integration notes:
  - The game loads 'glb/farm-house/farm-house.glb' via GLTFLoader, auto-fits it
    to ~2.2 world units and places it on the floating sand island (right side).
  - Until this file exists, a procedural low-poly fallback house is rendered.
